# Summary Notes Rules

>[!CAUTION] These are non-negotiable, VCAA rules
> 
> - Must be a single, bound reference book
> - Pages cannot be perforated or loose
> - No fold-out pages or overhanging tabs. Everything must lay flat and not extend beyond the edges of the book
>
> **If you bring a reference that doesn't comply with these rules, we will rip things out of it until it does.**
>
> That is better than letting you walk in with a breach of exam rules.

# Dumb ideas

- **Do not** duct-tape two books together
- **Do not** staple a set of loose pages together
- **Do not** include fold-out pages or tabs
- **Do not** leave sticky notes in your book

> These are mentioned because they have all been seen multiple times. Don't be the person in tears before the exam because we have heartlessly ripped your book apart.

# Summary Notes Strategy

<v-click>

- You have to be able to find things. Too long becomes useless
- Use colour, diagrams, tables and headings to make things easy to find
- Include worked examples when you need them
  - but only when you need them
- If you know you don't need it, get rid of it.

</v-click>

# Timing and Order Strategy

- Data (Stats) is twice as long as other topics
- 90 minutes is essentially split into 5 x 18 minute blocks
- You should complete your most confident topic first
- If your time for a given topic is up before you finish it:
  - Move on to the next one
  - Come back once you finish

This is because each topic has easier marks at the start. Your time is always better spent on marks you know you can get, rather than struggling with harder marks that may not be worth it.
