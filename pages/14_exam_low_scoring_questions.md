# Low Scoring Subtopics

Some question types are low scoring because of the topic.

- **Data**: Transformations (especially predicting from a transformed equation), Calculating seasonal indices
- **Recursion and Financial**: Loan Final Repayments, Nominal vs Effective Interest Rates, Changing terms mid-way through a loan or investment
- **Matrices**: Multi-step transition matrix problems, Matrix properties
- **Networks**: Flow problems, Crashing a network

# Low Scoring Question Types

Some trends exist in the types of questions that can be low scoring:

- **Difficult wording**: Some questions are low scoring because of how they are worded - leading to misinterpretation from students. 
> The lowest scoring question across 2024-2026 (3%) was a data question that asked *"What is the smallest possible number of Wgold heights lower than 1.85m?"* Most people gave the answer 1.67, which was the smallest height, instead of **how many** heights there were below 1.85m (1).
- **Awkward answers**: Rounding, failure to change a scientific notation answer (eg. 1.05E-4) to a decimal (0.000105), or failing to give an answer in the correct units (eg. $ instead of cents) lead to otherwise simple questions being relatively low scoring.
- **Failure to follow mathematical (or VCAA) conventions**: Use of casual or imprecise language caused some problems to be low scoring. When a template for answering questions exists: it is very important to follow it!

# Data: Trends in Low Scoring Questions

- CAS Calculator Exponent Notation: 1.05E-4 vs 0.000105, 4.3E7 vs 43,000,000
- Missing negative signs: especially in $r$ (Pearson't correlation coefficient) 
- Incomplete explanations: 
  - coefficient of determination ($r^2$) referencing both the response and explanatory variables, not just one of them
  - Interpolation vs Extrapolation: "value is inside/outside the range of *explanatory variable*"
  - Informal description of time series features

# Recursion and Financial: Trends in Low Scoring Questions

- Mixing up Recurrence relations vs Rules
  - Recurrence relations use $V_0$, $V_{n+1}$ and $V_n$ to describe the relationship between terms in a sequence
  - Rules will use $V_n$, $V_0$ and $n$
- Nominal vs Effective Interest rates
- Mid-term changes to loans or investments: these are just longer and more complex

# Matrices: Trends in Low Scoring Questions

- Transition models 
  - Multi-step transition models
  - Understanding what is happening: subtracting departing elements when finding a net state
  - Long term calculations
  - Working backwards from a final state to find an earlier state
- Labelling diagrams: arrowheads on directed graphs
- Understanding specific types of matrices: diagonal, permutation, identity

# Networks: Trends in Low Scoring Questions

- Crashing a network: can be complex, takes time
- Network flow problems: might not find the minimum cut

# Using your knowledge of low scoring questions

::left::

## ⬆️ Maximise your opportunities on questions you know you can do

- Practice specific question types as much as you can
- Use your notes for reminders on the common errors you'll avoid, and for templates for answering questions
- Practice identifying the tricks that make students give the wrong answers. Highlight keywords.

::right::

## 🤔 Make good decisions about questions you struggle with

- Have a clear idea of questions you will leave until last
- Notice when you have spent too long on a question
- If you have struggled with a question - put it out of your mind and move on. 

> Don't let one question ruin any others
